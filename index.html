What timescale does "Default Capacity (FTE)" represent?

FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) is a continuous headcount rate — it is identical for both Weekly and Monthly views. For example, 1.0 FTE means 1 full-time person working every week (and every month). Under the hood, PulsePlan stores each week's FTE rate and automatically converts it to Days and Hours based on your conversion settings (1.0 FTE = 5d/wk = 40h/wk; a 4-week month = 20d / 160h, a 5-week month = 25d / 200h).

Conversion Factors & Health Thresholds

Controls automatic conversion between FTE, Person-Days, and Hours across Weekly and Monthly views.

FTE Timescale Reference: Default FTE is a time-invariant headcount rate (1.0 FTE = 1 full-time employee). In Weekly view, 1.0 FTE equals Workdays/Week. In Monthly view, 1.0 FTE equals Workdays/Week × number of ISO weeks in that month (4 or 5), ensuring exact pro-rata consistency when toggling between Months and Weeks.
